Lantern Hand

Hold out your open hand and it becomes a lantern. A small paper traveller called Wick crosses your real room toward its light.

Checking for mixed reality…

Before you press Enter

  1. Sit where you can see your table or couch.
  2. Put your controllers down.
  3. Press Enter, then hold one hand out, palm open.
